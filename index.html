Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive my ticket after booking? −

Once your booking is confirmed, your ticket stub is instantly generated and stored under your My Bookings section. You can view your unique ticket ID and booking breakdown anytime.

Can I transfer or cancel my ticket? +

Tickets can be managed directly through your account dashboard. For cancellations or name transfers, please reach out to our 24/7 support team or admin.

How do organizers add new events? +

System Administrators can access the dedicated Admin Management Panel to create, edit event pricing/seats, or remove past events in real time.

Is the portal deployment containerized? +

Yes! The entire application is containerized using Docker, deployed on Kubernetes (Minikube) with zero-downtime rolling updates, and managed via automated Jenkins CI/CD pipelines.
